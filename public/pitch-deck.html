The Trust Deficit

Problem Statement
Capital Misallocation Risk
Campaign creators receive 100% of the funds upfront, creating high execution and rug-pull risks.
Zero Post-Campaign Control
Donors have no visibility or say in project operations once the funding goals are met.
Inherent Inefficiency
No built-in accountability triggers to verify milestone updates before funds are fully cleared.
Stellar Crowdfund Slide 2 of 7

Donor-Protected Escrow

Solution
Milestone-Locked Funds
Funds are locked inside Soroban smart contracts and only released incrementally upon milestone approvals.
On-Chain Democratic Voting
Donors cast votes to approve or reject milestone reports. Releases require 66% supermajority approval.
Soulbound Reward NFTs
Donors receive non-transferable Proof-of-Impact NFTs dynamically minted upon successful milestone approvals.
